ℹ️ Student Biometric Onboarding Note:

You do not need to take photos of the student now. Once registered, the student will log in to their Student Portal using their Student ID. Upon login, an interactive prompt will automatically guide them to scan their 5 face samples using their device camera.

Biometric Enrollment Checklist

  • Manager creates student record with Student ID & Course.
  • Student logs in via Student Portal with Student ID.
  • Student receives prompt to capture 5 facial frames.
  • LBPH model automatically trains upon 5th sample.
  • Student status changes to Verified / Ready.
