Student Support

Verascan Attendance System

Everything you need to know about how the student portal works, how attendance is marked, and what to do when you need help using the system.

How the app works

Verascan is a face-based attendance system built for student check-ins. Instead of manual signatures or roll calls, students log in, verify their identity with a face scan, and the system records attendance only when they are in the allowed area zone.

Step 1 Sign in Use your Student ID and device credentials to access the student portal.
Step 2 Wait for session Your manager opens a class attendance session and sends a live signal.
Step 3 Verify your location Your device must be within the active area geofence set by the manager.
Step 4 Scan your face The app captures a live image and compares it to your registered biometric model.

What the student portal includes

  • Student dashboard with your attendance status for the day.
  • Live session status and countdown timer for class attendance windows.
  • Attendance history and records for your courses.
  • Device security profile to confirm your account is linked to one device.
  • Face enrollment progress so your biometric data stays updated.
  • Area zone information telling you the permitted attendance location.

How do I sign in to the app?

  1. Open the Verascan app or web portal.
  2. Go to the student login screen.
  3. Enter your Student ID exactly as provided by your school or department.
  4. Allow the browser to access your camera if prompted.
  5. If this is your first login from a phone, the system may bind your account to that device automatically.
  6. After successful login, you will be redirected to your dashboard.
Tip: If your account is already tied to another phone, you may need manager approval to reset or rebind the device.

How do I mark attendance?

  1. Check whether your manager has opened an attendance session.
  2. Make sure your phone is within the live area zone boundary.
  3. Open the Attendance page from the student portal.
  4. Allow camera access so the system can capture your face.
  5. Face the camera and wait for the scan to complete.
  6. If the system recognises you and your location is permitted, attendance is marked successfully.

How does the system know it's me?

The app stores your face samples when you first register. These are used to train the recognition model. When a new attendance session starts, a live image is compared against your saved samples. If the match is strong enough and your location is valid, the system marks you present.

The system also checks the device used to log in, the area zone boundary, and the active time window so fake or remote attendance attempts are reduced.

Why do I need to be in the allowed zone?

The manager sets a geofence (area zone) for class attendance. If you are outside that zone, the system will not accept your attendance even if your face is successfully recognised. This helps keep attendance fair and location-aware.

You can see the current active zone in the dashboard and the student portal, including its radius and center location.

What if I changed my phone?

If you lost your device or switched to a new phone, you can request a device reset from the student portal. Your manager will review the request before the account is unbound from the previous device.

This protects the integrity of the attendance system and prevents unauthorized device sharing.

Where can I see my attendance history?

Open your student dashboard and use the My Attendance or Reports section to view attendance records, check history by date, and confirm whether you were marked present or absent for a session.

This record is useful for reviewing class participation and making sure your attendance has been properly logged.

Troubleshooting guide

If the camera does not open

Make sure your browser has camera permission enabled. Then refresh the page and try again. If you are on a mobile device, check the browser settings and allow camera access.

If attendance is not accepted

Verify that the attendance session is still active, you are within the correct geofence, and your face was captured clearly. Poor lighting or angled face capture can affect recognition.

If you are not seeing the session

Your manager may not have opened an attendance window yet. You can still check the dashboard for session status and wait for an active attendance prompt.

If your account is not found

Confirm that you entered the correct Student ID. If it still fails, contact your manager or school admin to confirm your registration.

Support and security

Verascan is designed to protect real attendance. Each student account is linked to a device, face recognition is used to validate identity, and location checks help prevent attendance abuse. If you need support or believe there is an issue with your account, contact your manager or the attendance administrator.
